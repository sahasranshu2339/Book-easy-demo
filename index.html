BookEasy: online appointments for small clinics and salons

A booking system that replaces phone calls and paper diaries. Try the demo below: pick a service, a date and a time, then confirm.

Upcoming bookings

    Case study

    The problem

    Small clinics take bookings by phone and WhatsApp. Slots get double-booked, patients forget, and staff spend hours confirming.

    What I built

    A booking flow where patients see only free slots, plus an admin panel where staff view, confirm or cancel appointments. Email reminders go out automatically.

    How it works under the hood

    Django and Django REST Framework serve the API, MySQL stores appointments with a unique constraint on doctor, date and time to prevent double booking. The front end is a lightweight JavaScript demo for booking interactions and reminders.
